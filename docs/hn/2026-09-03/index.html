    Summary¶

    Today's theme: AI dominated the day: GPT-6 Astra's purported 98.6% ARC-AGI-3 result and simultaneous ChatGPT/Claude/Grok/Codex outages shared the front page with Nvidia's Hugging Face deal and fresh open-source releases.

    📌 Suggested Reads¶

    • GPT-6 Astra — GPT-6 Astra is the biggest AI story of the day, and the reported ARC-AGI3 jump from ~30% to 98.6% is the key claim worth evaluating.
    • Qwen 3.8 27B available on Cerebras at 1500 tokens/s — Qwen 3.8 27B at 1,500 tokens/s on Cerebras is a concrete, fast open-weight model worth trying for coding-agent and high-throughput workloads.

    • Which tools do Claude, Codex and Cursor choose? We measured 17k runs to find out — A 17,000-run measurement of how Claude, Codex, and Cursor pick tools is useful for anyone building or evaluating coding agents.
