Work-based flushing exists to batch work to amortize overhead. It allows you to tune how much time you spend in overhead in a work period. However, this can result in unbounded delay.

Time-based flushing targets a worst-case latency in low throughput environments. This ensures you do not get unbounded delays. Interestingly, this also allows you to tune how much time you spend in overhead in a work period.
